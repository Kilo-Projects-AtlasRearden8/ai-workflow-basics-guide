Keeping Humans in the Loop

Chapter 3 of the AI Workflow Basics Guide — review checkpoints and human judgment.

AI is fast, confident, and sometimes wrong. Keeping a human in the loop means a person reviews, approves, or changes AI output before it is used for anything real. The AI does the draft work. The human makes the final call.

You do not need to review everything. You need to review the things that matter.

When human review matters most

Review AI output when any of these are true:

  • It goes to other people. (Email, newsletter, social media, a letter.)
  • It involves money. (Invoice, budget, reimbursement, pricing.)
  • It involves health. (AI can explain general health topics for education. It must never give medical advice, diagnose, or suggest treatment. A human professional must always review anything health-related.)
  • It is a legal or official document. (Contract, form, filing.)
  • It is about a real person. (Names, addresses, personal details.)
  • You would be embarrassed if it were wrong.

For low-stakes things — brainstorming names, exploring an idea, drafting a joke — you can skip formal review and just read it yourself.

The review checkpoint workflow

Add a human check between steps of your prompt chain.

Step 1: AI drafts
      ↓
Human check: read, fix, approve (or rewrite)
      ↓
Step 2: AI continues from the approved draft
      ↓
Human check: final read before sharing
      ↓
Done

If the check fails, send the step back with notes. Do not let errors flow into the next step.

Step-by-step: how to review AI output

  1. Read it out loud. Your ear catches things your eye skips.
  2. Check the facts. Names, dates, numbers, prices, addresses. AI makes these up more often than you think.
  3. Check the tone. Does it sound like you? Would you say this to a friend?
  4. Check for medical content. If it mentions health, symptoms, or treatment, it needs review by a qualified professional. AI output here is education only.
  5. Mark it. Write "approved," "edited," or "needs redo" and the date.

What to never let AI do alone

Never let AI publish or send the following without a human reading every word:

  • Anything with someone else's personal data
  • Anything about health, medication, or symptoms (education only, never advice)
  • Bills, invoices, or financial records
  • Anything going to a client, customer, or patient
  • Anything posted under your name or your business's name
A simple human-in-the-loop rule.
If you would not sign your name to it without reading it, do not let AI send it without reading it.
