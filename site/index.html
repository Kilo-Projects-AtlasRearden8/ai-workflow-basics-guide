Welcome

This is a beginner-friendly manual on simple AI workflows. It is written plain, step by step, with no assumptions and no jargon you do not need.

You will learn how to:

  • Choose your first AI tools without spending money you do not need to spend
  • Chain prompts together so one AI answer feeds the next
  • Keep a human in the loop so nothing important goes out unchecked
  • Use a printable planner to map your first real workflow

How to use this guide

  1. Read the chapters in order, or jump to the one you need.
  2. Try each step with a tool you already have.
  3. Print the planner and fill it in by hand before your first real workflow.
  4. Use the prompt chain tool (browser-based, nothing leaves your computer) to practice chaining.
Local first, no keys. Everything in this guide works without creating accounts, buying subscriptions, or pasting API keys into files. The prompt chain tool runs entirely in your browser and stores nothing on a server.
